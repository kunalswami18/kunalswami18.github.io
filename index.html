01 About

I'm a researcher working on generative AI and scene understanding: image editing and restoration, depth estimation, and vision-language models.

At Samsung Research, Bengaluru, I lead the Visual Generative AI team. Our work reaches Galaxy flagship phones, from Portrait Mode depth to Galaxy AI editing features. I'm also a PhD scholar at IIT Kharagpur, where I work on efficient video understanding for streaming and long-form video.

My work has won best-paper recognition and challenge awards at IROS, ECCV, CVPR and Samsung's global paper contest. Outside research, I contribute to open source (OpenCV) and enjoy competitive programming.
